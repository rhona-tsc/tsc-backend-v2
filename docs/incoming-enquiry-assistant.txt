BAND MATCHING — CONNECTED IN THIS REVISION
The read-only search uses approved/live, non-test catalogue entries and only public names, genre tags, lineup references/sizes and instrument labels. It does not load musician contacts, names or fees. It normalizes R&B/RnB, named sizes such as trio, size ranges and instrument aliases. Styles may be alternatives or all required. Size and instruments must belong to the same lineup. A brass section requires at least two separate horn players; one multi-instrumentalist cannot occupy two simultaneous horn roles. Blank unused member rows and managers do not count as performers.
The catalogue has 24 published acts and 75 lineups. Three Soul Allegiance lineups have declared size/performer-count inconsistencies. The assistant flags them for staff verification and does not present their recorded size as verified. It can still collect a named-band enquiry for review. Catalogue data was not silently changed.
The assistant returns at most three matching bands. Unknown constraints request clarification; no match does not relax the caller's preferences. Date availability, travel/location suitability and final pricing are not checked by this search. These remain for staff review.
A caller can save preferences without a band selection. Structured styles, preferredSize, mustHaveInstruments and selectedBands are saved on the enquiry board. Selected bands must have been looked up within that call, explicitly confirmed by the caller and revalidated against current catalogue data and the confirmed preferences. Saved criteria must match the search that produced the selection; copying unrelated catalogue genres or dropping requirements is rejected. Equivalent style spellings are collapsed without broadening the caller's request. Model-supplied names/IDs are not accepted as authority. Optional lineup references must belong to that returned band and match the criteria. The confirmed choices are distinct from suggestions.
Each active voice connection has its own selection state; public lookup results are not shared between callers. Saves still upsert one board item per call. These tools do not send emails, texts, calls or payments. No quote or booking is confirmed.
