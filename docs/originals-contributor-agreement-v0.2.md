# Contributor reservation agreement — review copy

Version: originals-contributor-v0.2. This copy reflects the participant-visible wording reviewed on 4 November 2026. Commercial decisions and legal-review gaps are recorded in [the review notes](originals-agreement-review-2026-11-04.md).

## Reservation and deliverables

An advance place has no recording countdown until its round opens and the place activates. A recording reservation then lasts 24 hours; extensions require separate approval, up to three times. Provide at least one and no more than three aligned instrument stems, plus one listening mix combining them with the authorised reference. Reference access is restricted and may end when participation ends unless an accepted contribution or later role requires continued access. Reference material must not be used outside this project.

## Project scope and your material

TSC means The Supreme Collective. These terms cover your participation in the project and role identified when you reserve. The project owner is the owner recorded in the workspace; the owner coordinates selection and TSC administers the collaboration. You confirm that you may enter this agreement and have the permissions needed to upload and contribute your material for the uses described here. Tell TSC before uploading material subject to a publisher, label, sample licence or other restriction that could prevent those uses. Do not upload another person’s work or performance without the necessary permission.

## Permission to work on the project

You retain the rights you own in your contributions and any pre-existing material. You give TSC, the project owner and authorised project participants a non-exclusive permission to store, securely play, copy, align, edit and combine your submitted material only as reasonably needed to evaluate and develop this project. Approved mix and mastering participants may receive controlled source downloads for that purpose. This permission does not transfer copyright, appoint a publisher, waive moral rights or authorise release, public performance, commercial distribution, use on another record or AI training. Those uses require the necessary separate permissions. Rejection, withdrawal and accepted contributions are handled as set out below.

## Confidentiality and restricted use

Keep unreleased music, reference files, private messages and participant information confidential. Use project material only for this collaboration. Do not reuse it on other records, use it for DJ performances or AI/model training, or redistribute it outside the project without the relevant written permission. You may share only what is reasonably necessary with professional advisers who are bound to keep it confidential, or where disclosure is required by law or needed to report unlawful conduct. Confidentiality does not cover information already lawfully public, already lawfully known to you, or independently developed without project material. Protection of remaining confidential information continues after participation ends.

## Selection, partial use and rejected material

A reservation, upload or submission does not guarantee selection, a paid fee or future royalties. The owner may select a whole take or identified parts of a take; the selected files and any time ranges must be recorded. Multiple selected stems, takes or ranges by the same person in one role count as one accepted role contribution for the master split, rather than extra shares per file or second of audio. Rejected material remains its contributor’s property and must not be used in later project versions or distributed. Later reinstatement requires a recorded decision, the necessary permission and review of the contributor’s credit and royalty position.

## Master income and deductions

The master-income allocation is 20% to TSC (The Supreme Collective), 20% to the project owner, 40% divided equally across accepted contributor-role contributions, 10% to the selected mix producer and 10% to the selected mastering engineer. One person can receive shares for more than one accepted role. The percentage for an individual contributor can change as other roles are accepted, until the final split is confirmed. These are allocations of the agreed distributable master income, not percentages of copyright ownership or of songwriting income. No unspecified cost is authorised for deduction by this agreement: any deductions, collection arrangements, accounting periods and payment obligations must be set out in the separate commercial agreement before release or monetisation. No earnings figure, stream count, advance or minimum return is guaranteed.

## Songwriting claims and proposed splits

Songwriting is separate from performance and master income. At submission, indicate whether you request a songwriting contribution as well as master royalties and describe the original composition you contributed. Under the project’s proposed unit framework, owner-supplied source material forms an initial writing unit and each accepted creative role adds a potential equal unit: claimed units go to the relevant contributor and unclaimed units are proposed for the original owner/songwriter. Without owner source material, the proposed writing split is equal between accepted roles claiming songwriting; if nobody claims, the proposed fallback is equal between accepted foundation roles. Listing a project, performing, mixing or mastering alone does not automatically make someone a songwriter. A checkbox or calculated fallback does not decide legal authorship, assign copyright, remove an existing claim or override a publisher’s or collecting society’s rights. Raise missing or disputed authorship before confirming the final split; it must be resolved with the affected rightsholders.

## Final confirmation and performer royalties

Master and composition splits must be recorded separately and confirmed by the affected parties before commercial use. A displayed estimate, an owner’s selection or a TSC workflow decision is not a substitute for a rightsholder’s required consent. Changes affecting an agreed share require the necessary further agreement and a retained record. Statutory performer rights and eligible performer payments, including PPL equitable remuneration, remain separate from this contractual master-income split. This agreement does not waive those rights or authorise TSC to collect them on your behalf.

## Expiry, release and concerns

You may release an unsubmitted reservation in the workspace. Expiry or release ends that reservation and makes its place available; it does not automatically cancel an accepted contribution or forfeit accrued, agreed royalty rights. Requests to retract submitted or accepted material must be raised with TSC in writing or through the project’s concerns process so the affected recordings, permissions and credits can be reviewed and the outcome recorded. No party may treat an access change or a missed message as an automatic transfer of rights. Keep project files secure and stop using material outside any continuing permission when your participation ends.

## Identity, records and privacy

Choosing anonymity does not remove TSC’s private identity and audit records. TSC may retain original files, decisions and agreement evidence where reasonably needed to administer the project, preserve agreed credits and royalty records, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes, including where material is hidden from ordinary participants. Access to identifying records must be limited to those who need it for those purposes; anonymity is not a promise that disclosure can never be legally required. Personal data must be handled under the applicable privacy notice and retention policy. Agreement acceptance is not consent to unrelated marketing or unlimited retention.

## Later stages and changes to these terms

Release, distribution, publishing administration, collection-society registration, sync, promotion and live activity require separate arrangements with the necessary rightsholders. These reservation terms alone do not authorise those activities or appoint TSC as your exclusive representative. The accepted wording, version, name, account, project, role and time are retained. Revised terms must be presented for fresh acceptance when required for new activities or activation of a waiting place; they do not retrospectively replace an earlier agreement or remove accrued rights merely because a new version is published. Raise a dispute with TSC in writing, identifying the project, material and outcome sought; this does not restrict any right to obtain independent advice or use an available legal remedy.

## Acceptance

I have read and agree to these terms, including the master and songwriting framework. By entering my full name and ticking this box, I intend to sign this agreement electronically for this project and role.
